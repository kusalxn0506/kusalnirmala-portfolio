KARATEPOSE-AI · FORM · PATTERN · DISCIPLINE

Kinematic Refinement of Coordinate-Based Human Pose Estimation via a Physics-Informed Filter Engine for Elite Martial Arts Analytics

Lightweight edge-deployable pose estimators (MediaPipe BlazePose v0.10.35 & YOLOv8x-Pose) suffer severe confidence collapse and spatial jitter when confronted with concurrent ballistic velocities (>8 m/s) and rigid heavyweight garment occlusion (14oz / 400 g/m² Shotokan Karate Gi). Built during Year 1 of the BSc AI degree, KaratePose-AI pairs a controlled dual-garment benchmark across an elite cohort (N=3, 180 paired trials) with a 10-module Python architecture featuring a Continuous White-Noise Jerk Extended Kalman Filter (`07_ekf_filter.py`), Joseph-form covariance stabilization, and 14-segment de Leva (1996) Center of Gravity synthesis (`09_cog_tracker.py`).
